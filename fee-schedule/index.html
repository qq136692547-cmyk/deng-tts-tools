Common questions

What is the TikTok Shop US referral fee?

A flat 6% for most categories, 5% for fine jewelry and pre-owned. Unchanged since 2024-04-01.

Is there still a transaction fee?

No. The US commission policy defines one fee (the referral fee). Third-party guides read payment processing as sitting inside that 6%.

How is FBT storage billed?

Per cubic foot per day, on sellable and defective units alike. First 60 days free, then 8 tiers by days held, minimum 0.001 cu ft per SKU per day.
