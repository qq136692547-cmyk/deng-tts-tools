TikTok Shop FBT Fee Per Unit 2026: Every Weight Tier

Fulfilled by TikTok is the largest single line on most TikTok Shop fee statements — routinely three to four times the referral commission. It is also the line sellers model worst, because the tier is set by the weight of the whole parcel rather than the product.

Quick answer

In 2026 FBT charges a single-item order by weight tier, from $4.28 for a unit at or under 4 oz and $4.52 for 4–8 oz, up to $9.63 at 9–10 lb and $12.67 at 15–16 lb. Multi-item orders are charged per order, so the per-unit cost falls sharply on bundles — roughly $1.56 per unit on the lightest tier for a four-item order. Run your own weight in the fee calculator.

Weight matters more than price

Seller attention usually goes to price. The tier is the bigger lever. Take a single $30 product and move it through the tiers:

The price never moved. Only the parcel did — and the platform cost nearly doubled, from 20.3% to 38.1%. A 17-point swing on the same sale is larger than the effect of most pricing decisions, and it is invisible in any model that treats fulfillment as a fixed assumption.

The same logic runs in reverse. Trim packaging and move a product from the 4–8 oz tier into the sub-4 oz tier and you save $0.24 per unit — on a 100-unit month that is $24, for free. Move a 5 lb item down to 4 lb and you save $0.90 a unit, or $90 a month. That is usually worth more than any referral-rate negotiation.

Why multi-item orders cost less per unit

Multi-item orders are priced per order, not per unit. The first unit carries the tier rate and additional units in the same parcel add only a fraction of it. On the lightest tier, a four-item order works out to roughly $1.56 per unit instead of $4.28.

Indicative figures derived from the per-order pricing behaviour of the same rate card — your exact rate depends on the combined parcel weight, so check the tier your bundle lands in. The direction is unambiguous though: bundling converts FBT from a per-item tax into a per-order one, which is why 2-packs and 3-packs outperform single units in the same category on this platform.

This is also why "free shipping over $X" promotions work differently here than on Shopify. The incremental second unit costs you a fraction of the fulfillment of the first, so the bundle discount is cheaper to fund than it looks.

FBT vs self-shipping vs upgraded TikTok shipping

FBT is the default, not the obligation. Three fulfilment paths exist in 2026:

  • FBT (Fulfilled by TikTok). You send inventory to a TikTok warehouse; TikTok picks, packs and ships. You pay the per-unit rate above plus monthly storage. Orders ship with TikTok's own handling-time guarantee, which feeds into listing quality signals.
  • Self-shipping. You buy the label and ship. Usually cheaper per unit on heavy or oddly shaped goods, and you control packaging — but you own the handling time, the late-shipment risk and the customer service load.
  • Upgraded TikTok shipping. TikTok buys the label, you pack and hand over. Sits between the two on cost and control.

The comparison in our shipping cost guide shows where each path wins. The short version: FBT usually wins on light units and on any product where the shipping badge changes conversion; self-shipping usually wins above roughly 2 lb, where the tier rate outruns a comparable carrier label.

How to find your real tier

Weigh the packed parcel, not the product. Packaging, mailers, inserts, protective wrap and the label all count toward the shipped weight, and TikTok rounds up into the next band. Products that sit just under a boundary are worth re-specifying:

  • A 7.8 oz parcel is billed at the 8–12 oz rate ($4.70). Shaving 0.3 oz of packaging drops it to the 4–8 oz rate ($4.52).
  • A 1 lb 1 oz parcel is billed in the 1–2 lb band ($5.71). At 15.9 oz it would be billed at $5.10.
  • Boundary products are the highest-return packaging projects on the platform, because the saving is permanent and applies to every unit you ever sell.

Watch out for the other side too: a heavier parcel also raises your own inbound freight and your storage cost per unit. Both are inputs in the profit calculator, which prices the full stack including returns and ad spend.

Frequently asked questions

How much does FBT cost per unit on TikTok Shop in 2026?

FBT fulfillment is charged per unit by weight. For a single-item order the rate runs from $4.28 for a unit at or under 4 oz, through $4.52 at 4–8 oz and $5.10 at 12–16 oz, up to $9.63 at 9–10 lb. Heavier tiers continue climbing to $12.67 at 15–16 lb. Multi-item orders are charged per order, so the effective cost per unit is lower.

Is FBT mandatory for TikTok Shop sellers?

No. Fulfilled by TikTok is the default option and the one most sellers use because it carries the shipping badge and handling-time benefits, but sellers can fulfill themselves or use upgraded TikTok shipping instead. The trade-off is between the per-unit FBT rate and doing the pick, pack and ship work yourself.

Why is my FBT fee higher than the rate card says?

The tier is set by the shipped weight of the whole parcel, not the weight of the product alone. Packaging, inserts, boxes and any protective material count. A product that weighs 6 oz but ships in a 9 oz parcel is billed in the 8–12 oz tier.

Does FBT charge less for multi-item orders?

Yes. Multi-item orders are priced per order rather than per unit, so the second and later units in the same parcel cost far less than the first. On the lightest tiers the effective rate for a four-item order can fall to roughly $1.56 per unit.

Is FBT cheaper than Amazon FBA?

The headline rates are in a similar range for light units, and TikTok Shop's referral fee is lower than Amazon's typical category rate. The difference shows up in commission-on-shipping and in creator commission, which has no Amazon equivalent. Model the same SKU on both platforms rather than comparing rate cards.
