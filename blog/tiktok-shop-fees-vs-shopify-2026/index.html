TikTok Shop Fees vs Shopify in 2026: A Structure Comparison

"TikTok takes 6% and Shopify takes 2.9%" is the comparison most sellers make, and it is not a comparison at all. One is a commission on sales. The other is a subscription plus a payment-processing fee. They are charged for different things, and they behave in opposite ways as your order value moves.

Quick answer

Shopify takes 0% commission on your sales. On the Basic plan at $29/month it charges 2.9% + 30¢ through Shopify Payments — about $1.17 on a $30 order. TikTok Shop charges a 6% referral fee ($1.80 on the same order) with no monthly fee. So Shopify is cheaper per transaction, and TikTok Shop is cheaper in total, because it supplies the audience. Shopify figures are 2.9% + $0.30 plus the $0.06 subscription share. Two patterns matter. First, TikTok Shop is a flat 6% of the buyer’s total payment regardless of order size, so its cost grows in a straight line. (The cost-per-unit table above assumes no separately charged shipping, so the base equals the item price in those three rows.) Second, Shopify's fixed 30¢ component becomes less significant as the basket grows, so its effective rate falls from 4.9% to 3.3% across the range.

Now the part that changes the answer: the third-party gateway surcharge. If you take payments through a gateway other than Shopify Payments on the Basic plan, Shopify adds 2% on top. That turns the $30 example into $1.83 — more expensive than TikTok Shop. On Grow the surcharge is 1%, on Advanced 0.6%.

What neither column includes

Both comparisons above leave out the two largest costs of actually running a store, and leaving them out is how "Shopify is 2.9%" becomes a misleading conclusion.

  • Fulfilment. On TikTok Shop, FBT adds $4.28–$12.67 per unit on a single-item order — typically several times the referral fee. On Shopify you ship yourself, which usually costs a comparable amount per parcel but gives you control over packaging and carrier. This line is where most of the real money sits on both platforms; see our FBT per-unit breakdown and the shipping cost guide.
  • Traffic. TikTok Shop's marketplace and content feed place orders in front of buyers; on Shopify you buy that attention. Our GMV Max guide covers what ads cost inside TikTok, and the short version is 10–20% of revenue for most sellers. Add 10% of revenue to the Shopify column and the comparison inverts instantly.

Put concretely: on a $30 order, a Shopify merchant paying 10% of revenue for ads is carrying $3.00 + $1.23 = $4.23 against TikTok Shop's $1.80 referral fee — and then TikTok's own ad spend, if any, on top. That is why the honest comparison is not "which fee is lower" but "which channel brings me a buyer at a lower total cost".

Frequently asked questions

Is TikTok Shop cheaper than Shopify?

Not per transaction. Shopify takes no commission on sales, so a $30 order costs roughly 2.9% plus 30 cents in payment processing, about $1.17 on the Basic plan, against TikTok Shop's $1.80 referral fee. TikTok Shop wins on total cost because it brings its own traffic; Shopify charges less to process an order you had to find yourself.

Does Shopify charge a transaction fee on every sale?

Shopify takes 0% commission on your sales. What it charges is a monthly subscription plus payment processing through Shopify Payments. If you use a third-party gateway instead, Shopify adds its own surcharge on top — 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow, 0.6% on Advanced.

Why is the TikTok Shop referral fee called a commission and the Shopify fee not?

Because they pay for different things. The 6% TikTok Shop referral fee is a platform commission on completed orders, and it already includes payment processing. Shopify's charges are a fixed subscription for the storefront software plus a per-transaction payment fee. One scales with sales, the other partly does not.

Which is cheaper for a $15 product?

They come out close. On a $15 order, TikTok Shop takes $0.90 in referral fees while Shopify Basic takes about $0.44 in payment processing plus 30 cents, roughly $0.80 once the subscription share is included. The gap is far smaller than the difference in the audiences each channel reaches.

Can I sell on TikTok Shop and Shopify at the same time?

Yes, and many sellers do. TikTok's official Shopify app is free to install and syncs your catalogue, orders and inventory between the two. The additional cost is not the app but the 6% referral fee on any order that completes inside TikTok Shop, plus any ad spend you choose to run.
