TikTok Shop FBT Fulfillment Costs 2026: Complete Fee Breakdown

If you sell on TikTok Shop in the US, TikTok is pushing hard toward its own fulfillment services — and independent shipping was restricted in early 2026. The three approved logistics pathways are Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT), Upgraded TikTok Shipping, and Collections by TikTok (CBT). TikTok paused the mandatory rollout in February 2026, but FBT remains the most popular option because it's the closest equivalent to Amazon FBA.

Here's what FBT actually costs per unit, how multi-item discounts change the math, what storage fees look like, and when you're better off with one of the other options.

What Is Fulfilled by TikTok?

Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) is TikTok Shop's in-house fulfillment service. You send your inventory to TikTok's partner warehouses, and they handle storage, picking, packing, and last-mile delivery to customers. It mirrors Amazon FBA's structure, though the network is smaller and less geographically distributed (source: Dashboardly, Apr 2026).

The key advantage: FBT exempts you from late-delivery and out-of-stock performance penalties. The trade-off is that you lose control over shipping speed and carrier selection.

FBT Per-Unit Fees (2026)

FBT charges a per-unit fee for each item shipped, based on the order's weight tier. For a single-item order, rates start at $4.28 (≤4 oz); a standard 4–8 oz item costs $4.52, and heavier items scale up to $12.67 (source: TikTok Seller Center FBT Rate Card). This covers picking, packing, and shipping within TikTok's delivery network.

The official TikTok Seller Center rate card confirms single-unit fees start at $4.28 (≤4 oz) and rise to $9.63 for the 9–10 lb tier and $12.67 at the top 15–16 lb tier (source: TikTok Seller Center FBT Rate Card). Rates vary by weight tier and negotiated terms.

Third-party guides cite starting rates of $4.28 per unit depending on weight tier (source: Printify, Jun 2026). For the examples in this guide, we'll use the $4.52 rate (standard 4–8 oz single item), which covers most standard-weight products.

Multi-Item Discounts

Effective January 12, 2026, TikTok reduced multi-unit fulfillment fees for orders with 2, 3, and 4+ items. The July 13, 2026 rate card keeps the discount across all weight tiers: a 4–8 oz item costs $2.91/unit in 2-item orders, $2.36/unit in 3-item orders, and $1.93/unit in 4+ item orders — versus $4.52 shipped alone (source: TikTok Seller Center FBT Rate Card).

Storage Fees

The first 60 days of storage are free. From day 61, FBT bills by the cubic foot per day, and the rate rises with how long the inventory has been sitting. The rate card effective December 15, 2025:

Storage daysRate (per cu ft per day)
30 days or lessFree
31–60 daysFree
61–90 days$0.03
91–120 days$0.04
121–180 days$0.06
181–270 days$0.12
271–365 days$0.14
Over 365 days$0.27

Three details decide the actual invoice. Fees are calculated daily at 23:59 on the total cubic feet of all your units, including both sellable and defective units — damaged stock keeps billing until it is removed or disposed of. Duration is Outbound Time − Inbound Time, so the clock starts when the warehouse receives the shipment, not when the label was created. The minimum chargeable volume is 0.001 cubic feet per SKU per day, which only matters for very small items.

Put on the same basis as a monthly figure, the 61–180 day tiers work out to roughly $0.90–$1.80 per cubic foot per month, and the aged tiers above 180 days to about $3.60–$8.10. Amazon FBA publishes $0.78 per cubic foot standard and $2.40 in Q4, but FBA bills monthly and FBT bills daily — the two only compare once both are on the same basis.

One claim worth correcting where you see it: seller guides often quote a flat monthly per-cubic-foot figure and add that FBT has no aged-inventory charge. The figure itself is roughly the mid-tier rate converted to a month — but it stops there, and that is what misleads. The card charges by the day, and age is priced into it, so a unit held past 180 days pays several times the “typical” number and one held past a year pays nine times the day-61 rate. Other Fulfillment Options

FBT isn't your only option. TikTok mandates three fulfillment pathways:

OptionHow It WorksCostBest For
FBT TikTok stores and ships $4.28–$12.67/unit + storage Sellers who want hands-off fulfillment
Upgraded TikTok Shipping You pack, TikTok provides discounted labels ~20% below carrier retail rates Sellers with in-house packing capacity
Collections by TikTok (CBT) TikTok picks up from your warehouse ~30% below Upgraded rates Sellers with their own warehouse and volume

For most new sellers, FBT is the simplest option. Upgraded TikTok Shipping and CBT require more operational setup but can save 20-30% on shipping costs if you already have packing infrastructure (source: Dashboardly).

But TikTok's fulfillment network is smaller, which can mean slower delivery to certain regions compared to Amazon's sprawling warehouse footprint (source: hyperfocus.tech).

When FBT Makes Sense

Choose FBT when:

  • You're a new seller without packing and shipping infrastructure
  • Your products are lightweight (under 4 lbs)
  • You can maintain inventory turnover within 60 days (to avoid storage fees)
  • You want to avoid late-delivery and out-of-stock performance penalties
  • You're selling products that encourage multi-item orders (bundles, sets)

Choose another option when:

  • Your products are heavy (4+ lbs) — FBT's per-unit cost rises significantly
  • You already have a warehouse and negotiated carrier rates
  • You ship high volumes (CBT's 30% savings add up quickly)
  • Your products are oversized and FBT's storage rates eat into margins

Quick Summary

FBT is TikTok's default fulfillment option for US TikTok Shop sellers, but it's not a one-size-fits-all service. The math changes significantly depending on your item weight, order size, and storage turnover. A few key takeaways:

  • Single-unit FBT starts at $4.28 (standard 4–8 oz items cost $4.52), while multi-item orders cost less per unit ($2.91/unit for 2 items at 4–8 oz) — bundle strategically.
  • 60-day free storage is a real advantage over Amazon FBA, but only if you sell through within that window.
  • For heavy or oversized products, Upgraded TikTok Shipping or CBT can save 20-30%.
  • FBT has fewer standalone surcharge lines than FBA — no low-inventory or inbound placement fees, and no separate aged-inventory fee (age is priced into the storage rates themselves).
