178 Section 301 China Tariff Exclusions Expire November 9, 2026: Landed Cost Impact on TikTok Shop Sellers

⏰ Critical Deadline: On November 9, 2026 at 23:59 EST, 178 temporary Section 301 product exclusions expire. Chinese imports covering consumer electronics parts, molded plastics, fitness accessories, and specialized hardware will see tariff rates revert immediately to 7.5% or 25% on top of base MFN rates.

If you sell on TikTok Shop US and source stock from China, your landed cost formula is about to shift overnight. With de minimis already eliminated and FBT fulfillment charges fixed, an unexpected 25% duty surcharge on your factory invoice can turn a healthy 20% net margin into a net loss per unit. Here is the verified regulatory breakdown, the exact SKU math, and what you must execute before November 9.

1. What Happens on November 9, 2026?

In May 2024, the United States Trade Representative (USTR) concluded its four-year statutory review of Section 301 tariffs, granting temporary exclusions for 164 industrial and consumer equipment codes, alongside 14 COVID-related exemptions. These 178 exclusions allowed qualified imports from China to enter at a 0% Section 301 rate (paying only standard Column 1 Most-Favored-Nation duties).

Following a short extension granted in May 2026, the final transition window officially terminates on November 9, 2026 at 23:59 EST. The USTR has issued no notices of further renewal. Under Customs and Border Protection (CBP) entry rules, any formal or informal consumption entry filed after that timestamp will be assessed full Section 301 duties under HTS heading 9903.88.01 (25%), 9903.88.02 (25%), or 9903.88.15 (7.5%) depending on the product's underlying 10-digit tariff line.

Let's run the exact mathematics on a real-world product: a Kitchen Organizer Set selling on TikTok Shop US for $29.99, sourced in China at $6.00 FOB with $2.20 per-unit sea freight & drayage.

• FOB Goods Cost: $6.00
• MFN Base Duty (4.5%): $0.27
• Section 301 Exclusion: $0.00 (0%)
• Freight & Port Fees: $2.20
→ Landed Cost: $8.47 / unit
TikTok Shop Platform Economics ($29.99 sale):

• FOB Goods Cost: $6.00

• Section 301 Reinstated (25% on $6.00): +$1.50

→ Net Profit per Unit: $7.42 (24.7% margin) — -$1.50 profit direct loss per sale!

4. Busting the 2026 Tariff Myths

Many sellers and outdated logistics guides are circulating conflicting advice. As verified in TTCalc's Rate Updates Evidence Log, keep these two facts firmly in mind:

  • Myth 1: "Can't we just ship air parcels under Section 321 de minimis ($800)?"
    FALSE. The $800 de minimis exemption for Chinese goods was eliminated on May 2, 2025, and suspended for all countries on August 29, 2025. On June 24, 2026, CBP codified indefinite suspension under 19 U.S.C. §1321, and the Court of International Trade upheld it on August 13, 2026. Every commercial postal and courier shipment into the US is now subject to formal customs entries and duties.
  • Myth 2: "Don't we also need to add the 2025 IEEPA tariffs on top?"
    FALSE. In February 2026, the US Supreme Court struck down the executive IEEPA “fentanyl and reciprocal” tariffs as unconstitutional. Importers are receiving full refunds through CBP's CAPE mechanism. Several competing calculators still erroneously add 10%–20% in dead IEEPA fees. Section 301 and Section 232 were untouched by the ruling and remain active law.

Frequently Asked Questions

When exactly do the 178 Section 301 tariff exclusions expire?

The exclusions expire on November 9, 2026 at 23:59 EST. Any formal entry or shipment arriving and entering consumption after that cutoff loses exemption status and pays the full Section 301 duty rate.

Which TikTok Shop product categories are most affected?

The 178 exclusions primarily cover manufactured components, consumer electronics accessories, molded plastics, specialized hand tools, packaging hardware, and specific textile articles. Finished goods containing these parts will see duty rates jump from 0% back to 7.5% or 25% on top of MFN base rates.

Can I use de minimis ($800 threshold) to bypass the returning Section 301 tariffs?

No. Duty-free de minimis entry for China-origin goods was terminated on May 2, 2025, and suspended for all countries on August 29, 2025. CBP regulations of June 24, 2026 made this suspension indefinite under 19 U.S.C. 1321, and the Court of International Trade upheld it on August 13, 2026. Every import pays duty.

Should I add IEEPA fentanyl or reciprocal tariffs to my landed cost calculation?

No. The Supreme Court declared IEEPA tariffs unconstitutional in February 2026, and CBP actively refunds previously collected IEEPA duties through its CAPE program. Do not stack dead IEEPA percentages into your SKU costing.
