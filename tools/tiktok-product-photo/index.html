How to get a usable listing photo in four steps

01 / workflow
  1. Start from a real photo of the exact SKU. AI is good at changing the scene and bad at inventing the product. Shoot the item on a plain background first so the model has truthful shape, colour and material to work from.
  2. Describe the scene, not the adjective. "Beautiful product photo" produces noise. Name the room, the light and the finish: white ceramic skincare jar on a bathroom ledge, soft morning light, blurred eucalyptus behind.
  3. Pick a scene preset and an aspect ratio. Square for the main listing image, vertical for short-video covers. The preset only changes styling — it should never change the product.
  4. Generate, then reject. Create up to four variations and keep only the ones where the product still matches what will ship. One honest hero image beats four attractive lies.

What a TikTok Shop listing image has to pass

02 / checklist

TikTok publishes its own image guidance in Seller Center and updates it; treat the five checks below as the practical floor, not a replacement for the current policy.

  • It matches the SKU. Same shape, colour, material and quantity as the unit that will actually ship.
  • Text is legible and correct. Any label, dosage panel or warning that appears must be spelled right — garbled AI lettering is the single most common reject.
  • The background is bright enough to read on a phone. Most buyers scroll in daylight on a small screen.
  • Colour is still truthful. A saturated render that flatters the product in the gallery but disappoints in the box drives returns.
  • The benefit lands in about a second. If a shopper cannot tell what the product is and why it is worth a tap, the image is decoration, not a listing asset.

Five ways AI product photos go wrong

05 / pitfalls
  1. The model redesigns the product. Extra buttons, a different lid, a colour that does not exist. Reject anything that changes the item.
  2. Garbled text where a label should be. Clean Listing Mode filters most random lettering automatically, but always zoom in on the label before you publish.
  3. Props that change the offer. A photo with a bundle-looking arrangement tells the buyer to expect more than one unit.
  4. Over-saturated colour. Flattering in the gallery, disappointing in the box — and returns cost you the fee stack twice.
  5. Spending the whole quota on near-identical shots. Three genuinely different scenes beat ten variations of the same angle. Treat the free quota as a test budget.

FAQ

06 / questions
Can I use AI-generated photos on TikTok Shop?

Yes, as long as the image truthfully represents the product being sold. Use AI for backgrounds, lighting and scene styling; do not use it to invent features, quantities or labels. If a generated image changes the product, it does not belong on a listing.

Do I need an API key?

No. Visitors without an account can generate 5 images a day, and signing in with Google raises the personal daily quota to 20. The optional API key field is for sellers who would rather spend their own model quota.

What does Clean Listing Mode do?

It runs an extra pass that removes the random text and watermarks image models tend to add, which keeps listings closer to TikTok's content rules. It is on by default and still worth a visual check on any label or warning panel.

What aspect ratio should the main listing image be?

A square (1:1) works best as the hero image, because it survives both the grid and the detail view. Use 9:16 for short-video covers and 16:9 for banners and ad creative.

What is the biggest mistake sellers make with AI photos?

Letting the model redesign the product. Always start from a real photo of the exact SKU, and reject any output that changes the shape, colour, material, label or bundle contents. Accurate beats attractive.

How many images should I generate per listing?

Enough to cover the hero image, one lifestyle shot and one short-video cover — typically three or four. The free daily quota is a testing budget, not a target. One clean photo of the real product is the cheapest way to improve your results.
