Frequently Asked Questions

02 / faq

What columns does the CSV need?

The easiest method is clicking "Download CSV Template". The parser accepts: SKU, Product Name, Sale Price, COGS, Weight in oz, Creator Commission %, and Return Rate %. Column headers are detected automatically.

Is my product pricing data confidential?

Yes, completely. Unlike server-side SaaS tools, TTCalc's batch engine executes entirely client-side using JavaScript FileReader. Disconnecting your internet connection will not stop calculations from working.

Can I export the calculated breakdown back to Excel?

Yes. Click "Export Enriched CSV" to generate an updated spreadsheet with every fee line, net profit per unit, margin percentage, and GMV Max target ROAS.
