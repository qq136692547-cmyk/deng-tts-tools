Tools for TikTok Shop sellers

Eight focused tools for the questions that decide your margin: what TikTok takes, what you keep, what commission you can afford, what fulfillment costs for your box, what holding unsold stock costs, what your ads must return, and how that compares to Amazon. The calculators run entirely in your browser — no account, nothing uploaded. Photo generation runs on our backend; the calculators never touch a server.

Quick answer

All eight TTCalc tools are free and need no signup. The US fee stack we model as of September 29, 2026: a 6% referral fee (its published policy lists no separate transaction fee), a 20% refund administration fee capped at $5 per SKU type, and FBT fulfillment from $4.28 per unit (≤4 oz, single-item orders, rate card effective July 13, 2026).

The calculators: what each one answers

Six calculators, six questions. Your inputs carry over between them, so you can price a product once and trace it from fees to profit to commission to fulfillment to storage to ad floor.

  • TikTok Shop Fee Calculator
    What does TikTok take from this order?
    The full breakdown on any price: the 6% referral fee, FBT fulfillment by weight tier, refund administration, and optional creator commission — ending in the one number that matters, the percentage TikTok keeps. On the default $29.99 example that is $6.32, or 21.1%.
  • TikTok Shop Profit Calculator
    And what do I actually keep per unit?
    Adds your COGS, shipping, and ad spend on top of the fee stack, then shows net profit, margin, and ROI as you type. Pricing decisions start from the payout, not the list price.
  • TikTok Shop Creator Commission Calculator
    What commission can I afford to pay?
    Works backwards from your costs and a target margin to the highest creator rate your product can afford — with a 0–20% scenario table, sample-cost amortization, and TikTok's official 1–80% range as the only guideposts.
  • TikTok Shop FBT Calculator
    What does fulfillment cost for this box?
    Applies the official chargeable-weight rule — actual weight at or under 2 lb and 332 in³, otherwise the greater of actual and dimensional weight (L×W×H÷166) — then shows your tier fee and compares FBT against your own self-ship and 3PL quotes.
  • TikTok Shop Storage Fee Calculator
    What does holding this stock cost me?
    Prices unsold FBT inventory by the cubic foot per day against the official age tiers — free for the first 60 days, then $0.03 rising to $0.27 past a year — and re-prices the same batch at 30, 60, 90, 180 and 365 days so slow sell-through has a number.
  • TikTok Shop ROAS Calculator
    What ROAS do I need to break even?
    Turns your fee stack and return rate into an ad-efficiency floor — the return your TikTok ads must clear before a product makes money, corrected for returns.

Where the numbers come from

Every calculator reads from a single rate file, and each result shows three fields: the effective date, the date we last verified it by hand, and a link to TikTok’s own rate documentation. As of our latest check on September 29, 2026, the US referral fee is 6% for most categories and 5% for select fine-jewelry and pre-owned categories, and TikTok’s published policy lists no separate transaction fee.

Not every tool online agrees — some still charge a $0.30 line, one stacks a 1.02% “processing fee” on top, and several reported an 8% hike in August 2026 that we could not confirm. We track every claim and every change in a dated log with official source links: see the TikTok Shop fee rate updates, including every referral-fee claim we’ve checked. When TikTok’s own rate card moves, the log and every calculator change the same day.

FAQ

Are these TikTok Shop calculators really free?

Yes. All seven tools are free with no signup and no account. Calculator math runs locally in your browser; your inputs are saved on your device and never uploaded.

Where do the fee numbers come from?

From a single rate file we verify by hand against TikTok’s published seller documentation. Each result shows the effective date, our verification date, and the official source. The full change history is on the rate updates page.

Which calculator should I start with?

Start with the Fee Calculator if you want to know what TikTok takes from a sale, the Profit Calculator if you already know your costs, or the Creator Commission Calculator if you are setting an affiliate rate. The ROAS Calculator covers the ad floor.
