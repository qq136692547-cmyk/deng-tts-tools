On the default $29.99 product, TikTok Shop takes $6.32 — 21.1% in platform fees: a 6% referral fee ($1.80) plus $4.52 of FBT fulfillment, leaving a $23.67 payout. What each TikTok Shop fee actually is

01 / reference
[ 01 ]

Base commission (6%)

6% flat for all US categories, charged on the total the buyer pays — shipping included (TikTok Shop US commission policy). Payment processing is already inside this rate, so there is no separate processing or transaction charge. Jewelry and Pre-Owned sit at 5%.

[ 02 ]

FBT fulfillment

$4.28–$12.67 per unit by weight, single-item rate for items up to 16 lb (FBT Rate Card). This is a flat per-unit charge — it does not scale with your sale price, which is exactly why it dominates the stack on cheap items.

[ 03 ]

Creator commission

0%–30%, set by you, paid to the creators and affiliates who move your product. Charged on the sale price and stacked on top of everything else.

[ 04 ]

Refund admin + FBT return handling

On a refund TikTok keeps 20% of the referral fee, capped at $5 per SKU, and FBT adds $0.70–$1.00 per returned unit. Both scale with your return rate.

[ 05 ]

What is NOT in this calculator

Ad spend, FBT storage fees, inbound shipping to the TikTok warehouse, aged-inventory surcharges, removal fees — and the product cost you lose on a non-resellable return. Those live in the Profit Calculator.

Why the same fee is 36% on one product and 10% on another

03 / price sensitivity

The 6% commission scales with price. FBT does not — it is a flat charge per unit. Hold the 4–8 oz tier fixed and price becomes the single biggest driver of your effective take rate:

This is the arithmetic behind the well-worn advice that TikTok Shop rewards higher-ticket, lighter items. A $15 impulse buy hands TikTok more than a third of its price before you have paid a cent for the product itself; the same fulfillment cost on a $100 item is roughly a tenth. If your take rate looks alarming, check the weight tier and the price point before you start renegotiating anything else.

Five ways sellers mis-model TikTok Shop fees

05 / pitfalls
  1. Reading "6%" as the whole fee. On most sub-$30 items FBT is the larger line. The blended take is rarely under 20% — see the claim-by-claim table behind these rates.
  2. Assuming FBT scales with price. It does not. A cheap, heavy product can lose more than 40% before COGS.
  3. Leaving creator commission at zero. If your growth plan involves affiliates at all, 0% is a placeholder, not a forecast.
  4. Treating a return as "just the admin fee." This page models the fee side only. The product cost lost when a return cannot be resold is charged against COGS in the Profit Calculator — run both before you set a price.
  5. Forgetting that shipping sits inside the referral base. Commission is charged on price plus shipping, so "free shipping" is not free to TikTok — it is priced into your fee.

FAQ

06 / questions
Is the 6% referral fee all TikTok takes?

No. The 6% referral fee includes payment processing, so there is no separate transaction or processing charge — but FBT fulfillment ($4.28–$12.67 per unit), creator commission (0–30%) and refund-related fees all stack on top. On the default $29.99 product the blended take is 21.1%.

Does the 6% referral fee include shipping the buyer pays?

Yes. TikTok’s US commission policy gives the referral formula as platform commission = platform commission rate × (buyer payment + platform discounts − taxes), and the note under that formula says: “buyer payment includes shipping paid by the buyer, net of shipping discounts provided by the seller or TikTok Shop.” This calculator adds any shipping you charge the buyer to the referral base; the field defaults to 0, so a price with the shipping baked in changes nothing. This calculator does not model platform discounts or taxes, so if either applies to your order the real base will differ from the figure here.

Do I pay creator commission on top of everything?

Yes. Creator commission is set by you (0–30%); this calculator charges it as a percentage of the item price, stacking on top of the referral fee and FBT fulfillment. A product that is comfortable at 0% can flip thin at 25%.

Is there still a $0.30 transaction fee?

No. TikTok Shop US has no separate per-order transaction fee. The flat 6% referral rate that took effect on 1 April 2024 replaced the older 2% + $0.30 structure and includes payment processing, so the transaction line in the results panel reads $0.00 and is hidden by default.

What does FBT return handling cost?

FBT charges a per-return handling fee — $0.70 for non-inspection service and $1.00 with inspection — for each returned unit, so it scales with your return rate. Separately, TikTok keeps 20% of the referral fee on a refund, capped at $5 per SKU.

Why is FBT bigger than the commission on my product?

Because FBT is a flat per-unit charge while the commission is a percentage. On a $29.99 item, a $4.52 fulfillment fee is 15.1% of the price while the 6% commission is only $1.80. Raising the price dilutes FBT; lowering it magnifies it.

How do TikTok Shop fees compare to Amazon FBA?

On headline commission, TikTok (6%) sits far below Amazon's typical referral fee (15%). On the full stack, TikTok's 6% plus FBT usually still comes out ahead of Amazon's referral fee plus fulfillment, placement and low-inventory surcharges — but Amazon's fee includes Prime shipping and warehousing. The side-by-side calculator puts both on the same inputs.
