    Quick answer

    The default inputs ship an 8 oz product in a 10×8×6 inch box. The box measures 480 in³, so dimensional weight (480÷166 = 2.9 lb) governs and FBT bills the 2–3 lb tier: $6.27 per unit. Repack the same product in a 6×4×2 inch box and the official 2 lb / 332 in³ threshold applies — it bills at its actual half-pound weight, $4.52. The box alone is worth $1.75 per unit, about $175 a month at 100 units. FBT's eligibility ceiling is 150 lb and 108 in on the longest side.

    How chargeable weight works

    01 / method

    TikTok's rate card states the rule in two branches, effective May 1, 2026. This calculator implements them exactly as written.

    [ 01 ]

    Light and compact: actual weight only

    weight ≤ 2 lb AND L×W×H ≤ 332 in³ → bill actual weight

    Most small, dense products live here. If your unit is at or under 2 lb and its packaging stays within 332 cubic inches, the box size is irrelevant — the scale decides.

    [ 02 ]

    Everything else: the greater of the two

    chargeable = max(actual weight, L×W×H ÷ 166)

    Exceed either threshold and dimensional weight enters the race. Bulky-but-light products — pillows, dog toys, cosmetic sets — routinely bill two or three tiers above what their scale weight suggests.

    [ 03 ]

    The warehouse's scale wins

    The official footnote is explicit: “The actual unit weight is determined by FBT's warehouse during inbound processing.” Your kitchen scale is an estimate; their inbound scan is the bill. Weigh a finished, packaged sample before you list.

    Where the tiers come from

    02 / rates

    The per-unit rate card this calculator reads runs from ≤4 oz ($4.28, single-unit) to 15–16 lb ($12.67), effective July 13, 2026. Multi-unit orders of the same seller in one checkout drop the per-unit fee sharply — a 4-unit order lands near $1.56–$4.54 per unit depending on tier.

    Every tier and both pricing tables are listed in the FBT fulfillment guide. The divisor people get wrong most often: TikTok divides by 166. Some guides quote 139 — the common domestic-parcel divisor — which would inflate dimensional weight by about 19% and scare you out of tiers you actually qualify for.

    Reading the comparison

    04 / comparison
    [ 01 ]

    What FBT's number includes

    Pick, pack and ship from TikTok's warehouses, storage with the first 60 days free, and itemized return handling ($0.70 non-inspection / $1.00 inspection per returned unit). After 60 days, storage is billed by the cubic foot per day and rises with inventory age — the Storage Fee Calculator prices it, and the Profit Calculator takes it as an input.

    [ 02 ]

    What self-ship's number should include

    Your label plus packaging per unit — and honestly, your time: fulfillment, tracking entry, and the returns process all route back to you. If a comparison only works because your time is priced at zero, it is not a comparison.

    [ 03 ]

    What the 3PL number should include

    Your 3PL's pick + pack + ship quote per unit, plus any monthly minimums amortized over volume. Quotes vary wildly by size and season — enter the number from your actual rate card, not a marketing page.

    [ 04 ]

    Why these two rows are inputs, not constants

    TikTok publishes no self-ship or 3PL rates, and this site does not invent numbers. Any tool that hardcodes a "typical" 3PL fee is guessing on your behalf; here you bring the real quote and the math stays honest.

    Frequently asked questions

    05 / questions
    How does FBT calculate chargeable weight?

    Two official rules, effective May 1, 2026. Units at or under 2 lb whose volume (length × width × height) is at or under 332 cubic inches are billed on actual weight only. Everything else bills on the greater of actual weight or dimensional weight, where dimensional weight is length × width × height divided by 166.

    What is the dimensional weight divisor for TikTok FBT?

    166. A 10×8×6 inch box measures 480 cubic inches, and 480 ÷ 166 is 2.9 lb of dimensional weight. If you have seen a divisor of 139 quoted for TikTok FBT, that is the common domestic-parcel divisor, not TikTok's number — using it would overstate dimensional weight by roughly 19%.

    How large can an FBT item be?

    The official eligibility footnote caps units at 150 lb actual weight and 108 inches on the longest side. The per-unit rate card on this site runs from 4 oz to 16 lb; items above 50 lb moved to extended heavy/bulky tiers on July 13, 2026, priced on a separate schedule this calculator does not model.

    Is FBT cheaper than shipping orders myself?

    It depends on your negotiated label rates and your 3PL quotes, which is why the comparison rows take your numbers as input. What FBT bundles in: warehouse storage with the first 60 days free, and itemized return handling at $0.70 (non-inspection) or $1.00 (inspection) per returned unit. If the comparison only works because your time is free, it is not a comparison.

    Can smaller packaging really save a full FBT tier?

    Yes, when dimensional weight governs. The preset pair ships the same 8 oz product: in a 10×8×6 inch box it bills at 2.9 lb dimensional weight ($6.27), and in a 6×4×2 inch box it bills at its actual half-pound weight ($4.52). The box alone saves $1.75 per unit — $175 a month at 100 units.
