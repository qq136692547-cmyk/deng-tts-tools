Put a TikTok Shop calculator on your site

Every TTCalc calculator can be embedded with a single iframe. It is free, there is no sign-up, and no attribution is required — although a link back is always appreciated.

How it works

Add ?embed=1 to any calculator URL to load it without the site header, footer, ads or article text. Copy one of the snippets below, adjust the height if your column is wider or narrower, and paste it into your page. The calculator runs entirely in the visitor's browser; nothing is sent to us.

What you get

  • Eight calculators: fee, profit, creator commission, FBT fulfillment, FBT storage, break-even ROAS, TikTok vs Amazon FBA, and the AI product photo generator.
  • Live math, not a screenshot. Values recalculate as your reader types, using the same 2026 rate card as the main site.
  • Kept current. When TikTok changes a rate we update the calculators, and your embed picks up the change automatically.
  • Responsive. The layout is fluid, so a width of 100% works in a sidebar or a full-width article.

1. TikTok Shop Fee Calculator

Breaks the full platform stack out line by line: referral fee, FBT fulfillment, refund administration and cross-border fees.

2. TikTok Shop Profit Calculator

Net profit per unit after fees, COGS, shipping, creator commission, ad spend and returns. The tallest of the calculators because it models the most inputs.

3. TikTok Shop ROAS Calculator

Works out the ROAS your reader needs to break even after every fee, then shows what they keep at whatever ROAS they are reporting.

4. TikTok Shop vs Amazon FBA

The same SKU priced on both platforms side by side, so the reader sees which marketplace pays more per unit.

5. AI Product Photo Generator

Turns a product description into marketplace-ready photos. Note that generation uses a daily quota and may ask the visitor to sign in, so it suits a page where the reader is already engaged rather than a sidebar.

6. TikTok Shop Creator Commission Calculator

Solves for the highest creator commission a product can afford at a target margin, with a 0-20% scenario table. Pairs well with posts about affiliate rates and creator partnerships.

7. TikTok Shop FBT Calculator

Computes FBT chargeable weight under the official rule (actual weight at or under 2 lb and 332 in3; otherwise the greater of actual and dimensional weight, L x W x H / 166), then compares FBT with the site owner's own shipping rates. Fits fulfillment and logistics posts.

8. TikTok Shop Storage Fee Calculator

Prices unsold FBT inventory by box size, unit count and days held against the official per-cubic-foot daily rates, and re-prices the same batch at 30, 60, 90, 180 and 365 days. Useful anywhere you write about inventory turnover, cash flow or dead stock.

Notes before you paste

  • Height. The values above are what we measured at a 780 px column width. If your container is narrower the calculator stacks taller — increase the height, or set it with CSS and let the iframe scroll.
  • Title attribute. Keep the title in the snippet. It is what screen readers announce, and it is required for accessibility.
  • Ads. Embedded calculators do not display our ad slots, so nothing of ours will appear inside your layout.
  • No tracking of your readers by us beyond standard analytics. The embed loads the same analytics tag as the rest of the site; if you need an embed without it, tell us through the feedback link.
  • Prefer a plain link? All eight tools are free to use directly at ttcalc.shop/tools/.
