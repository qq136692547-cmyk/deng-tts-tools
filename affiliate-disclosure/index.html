Affiliate disclosure

TTCalc is free to use and always will be. Some of the tool links on this site are affiliate links, and this page explains exactly which ones and what it means for you.

Which links are affiliate links

Any link marked with a star (★) next to the tool name is an affiliate link; every affiliate anchor also carries rel="sponsored" in the HTML, so the disclosure is machine-readable as well as visual. Right now that covers one partner:

  • FastMoss — a TikTok Shop analytics platform. If you subscribe through our link, FastMoss pays TTCalc a recurring commission. That commission is paid by FastMoss out of its own margin; your price does not change.

What it costs you

Nothing. The price you pay FastMoss is the same whether you use our link or type the address yourself. Our commission comes out of what FastMoss already earns on a subscription, and it never changes what you are charged. We do not add anything to your bill, and we do not ask you to buy anything for us to earn a commission.

Why we recommend it at all

Because we use the same numbers on this site. The fee calculations across every TTCalc tool are built on published TikTok Shop rates — referral percentage, FBT fulfilment, storage tiers — and FastMoss is one of the tools we check against the official Seller Center figures. The FastMoss vs Kalodata comparison and the product research tools guide are where the recommendation appears. The comparison is written from published pricing and quotas on both sides, including the places where we could not verify a number.

What this does not affect

  • The fee figures in our calculators. They come from official TikTok Shop sources, linked in Rate Updates, and no vendor pays us to change a number.
  • The verdicts in our comparisons. A partner cannot buy a favourable conclusion, and where the published data is thin we say so rather than guess.
  • Our independence from TikTok. We are not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance, and recommending a third-party tool does not change that.
