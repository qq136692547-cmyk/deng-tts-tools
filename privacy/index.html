Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28.

Summary

TTCalc calculators process the data you enter locally in your browser. Other features, including AI photo generation, Google sign-in, and analytics, use backend and third-party services.

Local calculators

Financial inputs such as sale price, COGS, fees, weights, ad spend, and expected returns are processed in your browser. TTCalc does not require an account or send calculator inputs to its backend.

AI photo generation

When you use the AI product photo tool, your prompt and any image you provide are sent through TTCalc's Cloudflare Worker to an image-generation service. The Worker checks daily quota limits. Anonymous quotas are keyed by IP; signed-in quotas are keyed by a Google account identifier. No email address, phone number, full name, or raw JWT is used as a quota key.

Google sign-in

Optional Google sign-in lets us distinguish your personal photo quota from the shared anonymous quota. Google sends a verified identity token to TTCalc's Worker. The signed session is stored locally in your browser. You can sign out at any time.

Analytics

TTCalc uses Google Analytics 4 to measure aggregate page views, tool usage, referrers, and conversion events. We do not send calculator financial inputs, generated prompts, or images to GA4.

Advertising

TTCalc uses Adsterra to display ads. Adsterra and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. You can manage cookies through your browser privacy controls and review the Adsterra Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

The only third parties involved in running TTCalc are:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, CDN, and the Worker that proxies AI photo requests.
  • Google — optional sign-in (identity token) and Google Analytics 4 (aggregate usage).
  • Adsterra — advertising, which may set cookies or similar identifiers.
  • An image-generation provider — receives the prompt and image you submit to the photo tool.

TTCalc does not sell your personal data, and does not share calculator inputs with any of them.

Data retention

Calculator inputs are not stored — they stay in your browser tab and are discarded when you close it. Photo-generation quotas are counters with no associated content: anonymous counters expire daily, and signed-in counters persist only as long as your local session. Generated images are not retained by TTCalc after delivery. Analytics data follows Google Analytics 4 retention settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export personal data about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing (for example, rights under the GDPR in the EU/UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California). Because TTCalc holds no account database and no stored calculator data, there is usually nothing to export or delete beyond what your browser holds locally — clearing site data in your browser removes your local session and preferences.

To make a request, or to ask what data a specific feature touched, open an issue at the project repository. TTCalc does not knowingly discriminate against users who exercise privacy rights.

Children's privacy

TTCalc is a business tool intended for sellers aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and TTCalc does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has submitted data through the photo tool, contact us through the repository above and we will remove it.

Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies on TTCalc come from three places: the site's own language preference (stored locally), Google Analytics measurement, and Adsterra advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; blocking advertising cookies will not affect any calculator.

No financial advice

TTCalc outputs are estimates based on the rate tables and inputs you provide. Fee schedules change, and your actual settlement can differ because of promotions, category rules, returns timing, taxes, and shipping variables the calculators do not model. TTCalc is not a financial, tax, or legal advisor. Verify figures against your own Seller Center statements before making pricing or inventory decisions.

Who runs this site

TTCalc is an independent project, not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance, and not affiliated with Amazon. It is operated as a non-commercial reference tool. For questions about this policy, use the public issue tracker linked above.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top is revised. Material changes will also be noted on the site.
