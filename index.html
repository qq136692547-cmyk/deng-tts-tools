Hidden fee stack

What you actually pay TikTok.

The 6% seller-center number is only the headline. The real cost appears when transaction fees, fulfillment, and creator commission are included.

Real stack example
32.1%

The example most sellers miss.

A $30 product with the $0.30 transaction fee, FBT, and a 10% creator commission can carry a $9.62 total fee stack. That is 32.1%, not 6%.

FBT fulfillment

$4.28+

Fulfilled by TikTok. $4.28–$9.63 per unit by weight (single item). Default fulfillment option since early 2026.

Cross-border fee.

~1%

International orders add roughly 1%. This applies when seller and buyer are in different countries.

Creator commission.

0–30%

If you work with TikTok creators or affiliates, this comes from your profit and stacks on top of platform fees.

FAQ

Common questions.

TikTok sellers ask us most about the fee stack, the dashboard mismatch, and how TikTok compares with Amazon FBA.

Why is my TikTok Shop profit different from the Seller Center dashboard?

The TikTok dashboard shows the gross amount after commission, but does not separately subtract fulfillment, cross-border fees, or creator commission. Once those are included, real take-home is typically 10–15% lower.

Is 6% the only fee TikTok takes?

No. The 6% referral fee includes payment processing. The $0.30 transaction fee, FBT fulfillment, cross-border fees, and creator commission can be added on top. A $30 product with FBT and 10% creator commission can reach a 32.1% total fee stack.

Do I pay creator commission on top of TikTok’s fees?

Yes. Creator commission is paid from your profit and stacks on top of referral and fulfillment costs. That is why the calculator separates each fee instead of showing one blended number.

How does TikTok Shop compare to Amazon FBA?

TikTok Shop has a lower headline referral rate than Amazon FBA, but creator commission and fulfillment can close the gap. Model the same product side by side before choosing a platform.
